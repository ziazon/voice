# VO14.2 brief: the calendar, from a file the person exported

VO14.1 built the grants and the audit log. **This slice, VO14.2, is the first capability that reads
something:** the person picks a calendar they exported (`.ics`, one file or a folder of them),
Voice keeps that choice like the claude.ai download choice, and reads it **only** through the
sandboxed reader (`voice reader read-calendar`), **only** under a `calendar.read` grant, **only**
within the grant's window, and logs every read and refusal in the audit log. Nothing uses the read
yet (VO14.4, after V-Q12) and the app gets its screens in VO14.3; this slice gives the CLI
`voice calendar choose|show` so the read can be seen.

The VO plan's rules this serves (`.ai/DECISIONS.md` and the plan's *Always*): the broker reads only
a consented source; every grant is dated, scoped, revocable and logged; calendar is **local-only**
by default (`voice_core::ceilings::Source::Calendar`); a write is never a standing grant (there is
no write here).

## 1. Decided at pickup (V1; don't re-derive)

- **Time zones are converted, never guessed.** Every time is shown in the person's own local time.
  `…Z` is UTC. `TZID=<name>` is looked up in the system time-zone database
  (`/usr/share/zoneinfo/<name>`, tzfile(5) version 2 or later, the 64-bit data block); a name the
  database doesn't have (a Windows name like `Eastern Standard Time`, a `/mozilla.org/…` prefix)
  makes that entry *not understood*. A floating time (no `Z`, no `TZID`) is the person's local
  time. All-day dates (`VALUE=DATE`) stay dates. The file's own `VTIMEZONE` blocks are skipped.
- **The person's zone** is found **outside** the sandbox (the reader can't read `/var/db`, where
  `/etc/localtime` points): `TZ` if set and not empty (a leading `:` dropped; a value starting
  `/` is a path, take the part after its last `zoneinfo/`), else the target of `/etc/localtime`
  after its last `zoneinfo/`. The broker passes the name to the reader, which loads it from
  `/usr/share/zoneinfo` (`/usr/share` is readable under `reader.sb`). No name, or one the database
  can't load: refuse with `ZONE` (§ 8), never fall back to UTC.
- **Recurrence understood:** `FREQ=DAILY|WEEKLY|MONTHLY|YEARLY` with `INTERVAL` (1–1000), `COUNT`
  (1–100000) or `UNTIL`, plus: WEEKLY `BYDAY` (plain days, `MO`…`SU`, no numbers) and `WKST`;
  MONTHLY `BYMONTHDAY` (a list of 1–31; a month without that day skips it) **or** `BYDAY` with an
  ordinal on every item (`1TU`, `-1FR`; ordinals ±1 to ±5; a month without that weekday-ordinal
  skips it); YEARLY with no `BY` part, or `BYMONTH` equal to the start's month (the start's month
  and day; 29 February skips non-leap years); DAILY with no `BY` part. `EXDATE` (any number of
  lines, comma lists, date or date-time in the start's form) removes occurrences. A `VEVENT` with
  `RECURRENCE-ID` and the same `UID` replaces (or, with `STATUS:CANCELLED`, keeps as cancelled) that
  one occurrence. **Anything else is not understood:** any other `FREQ` or `BY…` part, `BYSETPOS`,
  `RSCALE`, `SKIP`, both `COUNT` and `UNTIL`, `RDATE`, `EXRULE`, `RECURRENCE-ID` with `RANGE`, an
  override that itself has an `RRULE`, an override with no `UID`, more than 500,000 candidate steps
  for one rule, an occurrence after the zone's last transition when its footer rule has daylight
  saving (a footer with no `,` is a fixed offset and is fine).
- **RFC 5545's own rules apply** (§ 3.3.10): `COUNT` counts occurrences before `EXDATE` removes
  any; `DTSTART` is the first occurrence even when it doesn't match `BYDAY`/`BYMONTHDAY`; `UNTIL` is
  inclusive and compared as a UTC instant when `DTSTART` has a time (convert the `UNTIL` value the
  same way as `DTSTART`: `Z` as UTC, otherwise in the start's zone), as a date for an all-day start.
  A wall time in a spring-forward gap moves forward by the gap; one in a fall-back hour is the first
  (§ 3.3.5). Recurrences are expanded in the start's own frame (its zone's wall clock, UTC, or local
  for floating), then converted.
- **What comes out of an entry:** title (`SUMMARY`), place (`LOCATION`), start, end, all-day, and
  status (`TENTATIVE`, `CONFIRMED`, `CANCELLED`, matched without letter case; anything else or
  missing is none). **Never** `DESCRIPTION` (it often holds meeting links and codes), `ATTENDEE`,
  `ORGANIZER`, `URL`, `CONTACT`, `COMMENT`, `ATTACH`, `X-…` or any other property: they are not
  parsed into anything and never leave the parser.
- **End:** `DTEND`, else `DURATION` (`P…W` or `P…DT…H…M…S`, no sign or `+`; `-` is not understood),
  else an all-day start lasts one day and a timed one is zero length. `DTEND` before `DTSTART`, or a
  date `DTEND` with a date-time `DTSTART` (or the reverse), is not understood. An occurrence lasts as
  long as the first one, measured in UTC for timed and in days for all-day.
- **The window is the grant's,** in local days: from `today − past_days` to `today + next_days`,
  both days included. An occurrence is in it when it **overlaps**: its start's local date is on or
  before the last day, and its end's local date is on or after the first day (for all-day, the end
  date is exclusive in the file, so its last day is `DTEND − 1`; for timed, an end at exactly
  00:00 counts as the day before, unless start = end).
- **The text filters** (`voice_ingest::filters::Filters`, from `member.filters`) run on each title
  and place: when either is `personal` or `secret`, the occurrence is left out and counted.
- **Counts** (what the person is told): `privateLeftOut` is the number of occurrences in the window
  that the filters left out. `notUnderstood` is the number of `VEVENT` entries left out as not
  understood **that could touch the window**: those whose `DTSTART` can't be read at all, every
  recurring one whose `DTSTART` date is on or before the window's last day + 1, and every other one
  whose `DTSTART` date is within the window widened by one day each side (date read as written,
  before any zone conversion). Old history never counts.
- **Caps:** a folder holds at most 64 `.ics` files (more refuses with `TOO_MANY`); one file at most
  64 MiB and all together at most 128 MiB (more refuses with `TOO_BIG`); at most 200,000 components
  (more refuses with `TOO_BIG`); one unfolded content line at most 64 KiB (longer: inside a `VEVENT`
  that entry is not understood, outside one the file is bad); at most **2,000** events come out,
  earliest first, and `more` says some were cut. Titles and places are cleaned (§ 4) and cut to 200
  characters plus `…`.
- **A file Voice can't read at all** (not UTF-8 after an optional BOM, no `BEGIN:VCALENDAR`,
  `BEGIN`/`END` that don't pair, a `BEGIN:VEVENT` with no `END`) refuses the whole read with `BAD`.
  A bad entry is counted, never a reason to refuse the file.
- **Off macOS** the calendar read refuses with `MAC_ONLY_CALENDAR` (§ 8): it only ever runs in the
  sandboxed reader. Choosing a file works everywhere.
- **The reader re-checks:** it loads the grant itself (`grants::load`, read only, logs nothing) and
  uses **its** window, so a request can't widen it; it refuses a `today` more than one day from its
  own UTC date. The broker checks the grant first with `grants::require` (which logs a refusal),
  checks the reader's answer (§ 6), then logs the read with `grants::record_read`. If that log line
  can't be written, the read is thrown away and the person gets `LOG` (§ 8): no read without a trace.

## 2. The zone database: `voice_ingest::calendar::zone`

New module folder `builder/ingest/src/calendar.rs` + `builder/ingest/src/calendar/{ics.rs,
recur.rs, zone.rs, read.rs}` (`pub mod calendar;` in `lib.rs`, alphabetical). Use `dates.rs`'s
`days_from_civil`, `civil_from_days` and `days_in_month` for every date sum; don't write another.

```rust
pub const ZONEINFO: &str = "/usr/share/zoneinfo";
pub struct Zone { /* transitions (i64 UTC seconds), their offsets, the offset before the first, the footer's fixed offset if it has no DST rule */ }
impl Zone {
    pub fn valid_name(name: &str) -> bool;   // 1–64 chars; '/'-separated parts of A-Z a-z 0-9 _ + -; no empty part, no "." or ".." part
    pub fn load(root: &Path, name: &str) -> Option<Zone>; // invalid name, missing, not a regular file after following links, over 512 KiB, not TZif v2+, or malformed: None
    pub fn utc() -> Zone;
    pub fn offset_at(&self, utc: i64) -> Option<i32>;      // None only past the last transition with a DST footer
    pub fn to_utc(&self, wall: i64) -> Option<i64>;        // wall = seconds of the civil time as if UTC; gap: forward by the gap; overlap: the earlier instant
}
```

Parse tzfile(5): skip the version-1 block using its own counts, read the version-2 header and its
64-bit transitions, type indices, `ttinfo` (`i32` UT offset, `u8` isdst, `u8` index) and skip the
rest of the block; the footer is the text between the last two `\n`. Check every count against the
bytes actually there (no index out of range, no type index ≥ `typecnt`, transitions ascending):
anything off is `None`. Before the first transition, use the first `ttinfo` whose isdst is 0, else
the first.

## 3. The parser and expansion: `voice_ingest::calendar`

```rust
pub struct Date { pub year: i64, pub month: u8, pub day: u8 }           // YYYY-MM-DD; Display
pub enum Status { Tentative, Confirmed, Cancelled }
pub struct Event { pub title: String, pub place: Option<String>, pub start: String, pub end: String, pub all_day: bool, pub status: Option<Status> }
pub struct Calendar { pub events: Vec<Event>, pub from: Date, pub to: Date, pub private_left_out: usize, pub not_understood: usize, pub more: bool }
pub struct Window { pub today: Date, pub past_days: u16, pub next_days: u16 }
pub fn read(files: &[Vec<u8>], local: &Zone, zones: &Path, window: Window, filters: &Filters) -> Result<Calendar>;
pub const MAX_EVENTS: usize = 2_000;
```

- `start`/`end`: timed `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM` local; all-day `YYYY-MM-DD`, `end` its **last** day
  (inclusive). Sorted by start, then end, then title; then cut to `MAX_EVENTS` with `more`.
- `zones` is the database root (`ZONEINFO` in the reader; tests pass it too). Cache each loaded
  `Zone` by name for the read.
- **Content lines** (RFC 5545 § 3.1): split on `\n` (a trailing `\r` dropped); a line starting
  with a space or tab continues the previous one (drop that one character). Property name and
  parameters up to the first `:` outside double quotes; names and parameter names without letter
  case; parameter values may be quoted. Only `BEGIN`, `END`, `UID`, `DTSTART`, `DTEND`, `DURATION`,
  `SUMMARY`, `LOCATION`, `STATUS`, `RRULE`, `EXDATE`, `RECURRENCE-ID`, `RDATE`, `EXRULE` inside a
  `VEVENT` are looked at; a component nested in a `VEVENT` (`VALARM`) is skipped whole; every
  component other than `VEVENT` is skipped whole (a `VEVENT` inside one is not read).
  A second `DTSTART`, `DTEND`, `RRULE` and the like in one entry is not understood.
- **Values** (§ 3.3.4, 3.3.5, 3.3.11): `DATE` `YYYYMMDD`; `DATE-TIME` `YYYYMMDDTHHMMSS[Z]`
  (seconds 00–60, 60 read as 59); `VALUE=DATE` or an 8-digit value is a date. Text unescapes `\\`
  `\;` `\,` `\n` `\N`; an unknown escape keeps the character after the backslash.
- **Expansion** stops when a candidate's start (in its own frame) passes the window's last day + 2,
  when `COUNT` is used up, or after `UNTIL`; candidates before the window's first day − 2 are
  stepped over without converting. Each kept occurrence is converted to UTC, then to local with
  `local`, then tested against the window (§ 1).

## 4. Cleaning a title or a place

After unescaping: every `char::is_control`, `U+2028`, `U+2029`, `U+200E`, `U+200F`,
`U+202A`–`U+202E` and `U+2066`–`U+2069` becomes a space; runs of whitespace become one space; trim.
More than 200 characters: the first 200 plus `…`. An empty place is none; an empty title stays `""`.

