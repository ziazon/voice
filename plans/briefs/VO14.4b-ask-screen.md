# VO14.4b brief: the Ask screen

VO14.4a built Ask in the library and the terminal: `voice_api::ask(AskRequest)` sends one question
to one built assistant, with the permissions the person ticks this time; the privacy guard routes it
before anything is read (the calendar stays on the person's own machines by default); the reply is
cleaned, shown and never kept. The person chose this as the first place a capability's read reaches
a model (V-Q12, answer a). This slice is **the app's screen for it**: pick an assistant, type one
question, tick which granted permissions feed it this time, see where it may go, then the reply.
One new app command, `ask_assistant`, paired with `voice ask` in the parity table. Panels stay
unchanged.

## 1. Decisions already made (do not change them)

- **One app command, no library change.** `ask_assistant(request: AskRequest) -> AskAnswer` calls
  `voice_api::ask(request)` and nothing else. Its failures are already fixed sentences.
- **Where it lives:** a home card, like Panels, because it starts by picking an assistant. On the
  home view in `VoiceApp.vue`, a new card **between `<Assistants>` and the Panels card**, so Panels
  stays the last card. It opens a new view `'ask'` showing `Ask.vue`. No rail step.
- **Where it may go is shown before asking, from what's ticked** (§ 4.4). The screen doesn't ask
  the backend for the route (that would be a second command); the exact answer comes back after the
  ask as `said`, and a refusal comes back as the guard's own sentence.
- **Ticks are per question.** Every permission starts unticked each time an assistant is chosen;
  nothing remembers a tick.
- **Only permissions that are on can be ticked.** Off ones aren't listed.
- **Leaving is blocked while a question is out**, as Panels (§ 5), so an answer that is being paid
  for isn't thrown away.
- **All text through `{{ }}`**, never `v-html`; the reply keeps its line breaks with
  `white-space: pre-wrap`.

## 4. `src/screens/Ask.vue` (export it from `src/index.ts`)

No props. `const emit = defineEmits<{ running: [boolean] }>()`. Words in a new
`src/screens/ask/words.ts` (as `permissions/words.ts`); reuse `capitalFirst` from

### 4.1 The heading

`h2.card-title` `Ask one assistant`. Under it: `Ask one of your assistants a question and see how it
would answer. You choose what it may use, each time.`

### 4.2 Who to ask

- On mount, `listAssistants()`. Loading: `<p role="status">Loading your assistants…</p>`. Failure:
  `alert alert-danger`, `role="alert"`, `failureMessage(failure, "Your assistants couldn't be read. Close Voice and open it again.")`
  (NewPanel's sentence). None: `Make an assistant first.`
- A `<fieldset>` with `<legend class="form-label">Who to ask</legend>`: one radio per assistant
  (`label.form-check`, `input.form-check-input type="radio" name="ask-assistant" :value="slug"`, the
  name in `span.form-check-label`). An assistant without the person's permission
  (`!consent.agreed`) is disabled, with `<p class="text-secondary">Needs your permission first</p>`
  under it, as NewPanel. Nothing is chosen at first.
- Choosing an assistant: clears the ticks, the answer, `said` and any error, then loads
  `listPermissions({ slug })`. A response for an assistant that is no longer the chosen one is
  ignored (keep a request counter; the newest choice wins).

### 4.3 What it may use this time

Shown once an assistant is chosen.

- Loading: `<p role="status">Looking at this assistant's permissions…</p>`. Failure: `alert
  alert-warning`, `role="alert"`, `failureMessage(failure, "Voice couldn't look at this assistant's permissions. Try again.")`
  and a **Try again** button (`btn btn-outline-secondary btn-sm`) that loads them again. While the
  permissions are loading or failed, **Ask** is disabled.
- A `<fieldset>` with `<legend class="form-label">What it may use this time</legend>`, then, for
  each permission with `on: true`, in the order returned: a checkbox (`:value="permission.id"`,
  `v-model` on a `string[]`) labelled with `capitalFirst(permission.words)` (`Read your calendar`),
  and under it a muted hint `form-hint`: `` `${capitalFirst(permission.window)}.` `` (e.g. `From today
  to 14 days ahead.`; `window` is set whenever `on` is).
- For `calendar.read` only: when `calendarProblem` is set, the checkbox is disabled and the hint is
  followed by `<p class="text-warning small">` holding `calendarProblem`; else when `calendar` is
  `null`, the checkbox is disabled and that line reads `No calendar file chosen yet. Choose one on
  this assistant's Save page.`
- No permission on: instead of checkboxes, `<p class="text-secondary">`
  `{name} hasn't been given permission to read anything, so it will use only its decisions and your
  question. You can give permission on its Save page.`

### 4.4 Where it may go

A `<p>` with `aria-live="polite"`, shown once the permissions have loaded, built by
`whereWords(name: string, ticked: string[]): string` in `ask/words.ts`:

- nothing ticked: `Voice will send {name}'s decisions and your question, nothing else. They go to
  your own Claude Code, unless you've chosen to keep them on your own machines.`
- `calendar.read` ticked: `Voice will send {name}'s decisions, your question and your calendar for
  the days you allowed, nothing else. Your calendar is kept on your own machines unless you've let
  it go to Anthropic. Until you do, only a model server on your own machines can answer, and if
  none is set up, nothing is sent and Voice tells you why.`

`{name}` is the chosen assistant's `name`.

### 4.5 The question

- `<label for="ask-question" class="form-label">Your question</label>`, a
  `textarea#ask-question.form-control` (`rows="4"`, `aria-describedby="ask-question-count"`), and
  `<p id="ask-question-count" class="form-hint">{n} of 2,000 characters</p>`, where `n` is
  `[...question.trim()].length` written with thousands commas (`toLocaleString('en-US')`).
- Under it: `<p class="text-secondary small">Each question counts toward this assistant's daily
  spending limit. Voice doesn't keep the question or the answer.</p>`
- **Ask** (`button type="submit" class="btn btn-primary"`, in a `<form @submit.prevent>`) is
  enabled only when an assistant is chosen, its permissions have loaded, `n` is 1 to 2,000, and no
  question is out.

### 4.6 Asking

- On submit: `asking = true`, `emit('running', true)`, clear the answer, `said` and error; call
  `askAssistant({ slug, question: question.trim(), capabilities: ticked })`, where `ticked` is in
  the order the permissions were returned. While it is out: every radio, checkbox, the textarea and
  **Ask** are disabled, and `<p role="status">` shows `Asking {name}… This can take a minute.`
- Success: show the answer in `<div class="mt-4 text-break" style="white-space: pre-wrap" data-answer>`
  under an `h3` `{name}'s answer`, then `said` in `<p class="text-secondary small mt-2" data-said>`.
  The question stays in the box, so it can be asked again.
- Failure: `alert alert-warning`, `role="alert"`, `failureMessage(failure, "Voice couldn't ask this assistant. Try again.")`.
- Either way: `asking = false`, `emit('running', false)`. Also emit `running: false` in
  `onBeforeUnmount` if a question is out.
